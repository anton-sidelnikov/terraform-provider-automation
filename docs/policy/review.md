# Independent Review Policy

Policy ID: review  
Status: Adopted  
Version: 1  
Adopted: 2026-08-06

## 1. Independence

Reviewers receive frozen requirements, evidence, candidate output, and diagnostics without the author's hidden reasoning or conversation history.

## 2. Capability

The reviewer route is equal to or stronger than the author route. A model cannot approve its own output without an independent invocation.

## 3. Findings and repair

Findings are structured, evidence-linked, and actionable. Reviewers do not modify code. At most two repair rounds are allowed and every round is preserved.

## 4. Review checklist

- [ ] Frozen artifacts were reviewed independently.
- [ ] Reviewer capability met or exceeded author capability.
- [ ] Findings and repair history are auditable.
